A CLEAR ROLE IN EDUCATION

For the learner.
With the institution.

Padi helps people understand, practise and make progress. Runnerbot Education OS is intended to provide the institutional and governance layer around that learning experience.

You are using the personal experience. No school connection, school-approved knowledge, teacher dashboard or automatic human escalation is active here. This page describes the intended relationship, not a live institutional integration.

Padi is the learner’s space

Explanations, guided practice, revision, writing feedback and study planning belong in Padi. The aim is to help learners build understanding and independence, while supporting their teachers.

Education OS is the institution’s responsibility

Approved resources, curriculum configuration, access roles, institutional policies, auditing and human support pathways belong in a separately governed institutional experience. These controls need to be configured and verified before a school pilot.

What a responsible pilot needs

  • Verified institution identity, appropriate consent and clear access boundaries.
  • Approved learning materials with named owners and review dates.
  • A designated human safeguarding pathway with transparent responsibilities.
  • Minimal reporting, clear retention rules and no unnecessary sharing of private conversations.
  • Testing for accuracy, age appropriateness, learning progress and isolation between institutions.

Human authority stays central

Padi does not replace teachers, counsellors, safeguarding staff, examination bodies or professional judgment. An institution must tell learners what is monitored, who can see it and what happens when help is needed.
