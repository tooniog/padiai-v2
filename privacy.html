YOUR INFORMATION. YOUR CHOICES.

A useful Padi respects your privacy.

This page explains the current personal assistant experience. It is not a claim of regulatory certification or a promise that an AI answer is always correct.

Browser memory starts off. You can ask without a profile. If you choose “Remember on this browser”, Padi saves your preferences, your most recent conversation and practice results on that device. Anyone with access to that browser may see them.

What is sent when you ask

Your question, recent conversation context, selected preferences and any notes you paste are sent to Padi’s service and its AI provider to produce a response. Online research, when available, may send a search query to a search provider. Do not include passwords, payment details, identification numbers, confidential school or customer information, or private details about someone else.

What stays in browser memory

Memory is optional and specific to this browser. It can include your broad learning level, language, subject, goal, explanation preference, broad location, most recent conversation and practice answers. Pasted notes are not separately saved. An answer may repeat information from your notes, so check conversations before saving them. This is not a cloud account or cross-device backup.

Export, switch off or delete

Open Your preferences in the assistant. Export your current preferences, conversation and practice results, turn off browser memory to remove saved assistant data, or choose Delete my data to reset both saved data and the current session. New conversation clears the current conversation and pasted notes while keeping preferences and practice results.

The separate AI skills library may remember your chosen language and learning track, and keep a quiz score for the browser session. Delete my data also clears those library settings and scores on this browser. Assistant data export includes the assistant’s preferences, conversation and practice results.

Browser deletion does not remove information already processed by AI, search or hosting providers. Provider retention and service security logs follow those providers’ settings and policies. Avoid sharing sensitive data. If browser storage is unavailable, Padi keeps your work in the current session only.

Learning progress without invented scores

Practice progress counts the questions you actually answer and highlights topics to review. It is not an official grade, diagnosis or precise measurement of mastery. AI-generated practice can contain mistakes; ask a teacher or check course materials when something seems wrong.

Support with clear limits

Padi is AI, not a person or an emergency service. It can help you think through a concern and encourage appropriate human support. No safeguarding officer is monitoring this personal experience, and Padi cannot claim that it has contacted someone for you. Important health, legal, financial and safety decisions need reliable sources and suitable human expertise.

Institutions are a separate experience

This personal assistant is not connected to a school or an institution. A future institution deployment must explain its own access, safeguarding, retention and governance arrangements before use. It must not silently expose personal conversations to teachers or administrators. Read about Padi and Runnerbot Education OS.
